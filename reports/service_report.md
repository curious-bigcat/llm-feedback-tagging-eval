# Service patient feedback report

*Service: Forensic. 669 comments ("What was good?" 344, "What could we do better?" 325); 39 with high impact (4-5). Numbers from the semantic view SERVICE_SV; text written by gemini-3.1-pro (run 1).*

## Overview

Many patients express deep gratitude for the care, treatment, and support they receive, highlighting the positive impact on their recovery and safety. However, there are significant concerns regarding severe staff shortages, which limit access to activities and fresh air. Patients also frequently report long waiting times for medical and dental appointments, alongside issues with communication and feeling unheard by some staff.

## Themes

### General
*270 comments - 16% negative, 76% positive*

Many patients share overwhelmingly positive feedback, expressing gratitude for the excellent care, treatment, and support that has helped them progress. A few patients specifically highlight feeling safe and well-looked after, especially during the pandemic. Conversely, some patients report negative experiences related to poor organization, feeling that nothing gets done efficiently, and general dissatisfaction with the service.

#### Positive experience & gratitude
*190 comments - 1% negative, 99% positive*

Many patients express immense gratitude for the overall service, praising the treatment, support, and positive changes it has brought to their lives.

> "The help I received has helped me progress through a life sentence and for that I'll always be grateful."
>
> "My overall experience with the service has been very good"

#### Not assigned
*51 comments - 71% negative, 8% positive*

Some patients express general dissatisfaction, citing poor services, a lack of help, and a desire for better mental health support.

> "The general NHS services are terrible!!!"
>
> "And better mental health services, more support for prisoners with mental health."

#### Labelling not possible
*13 comments - 0% negative, 0% positive*

A few patients provided brief, neutral comments that could not be categorized.

> "Don't know."
>
> "No comment."

#### Feeling safe
*11 comments - 0% negative, 100% positive*

A few patients appreciate the staff for keeping them safe and providing reassurance during difficult times.

>

#### Organisation & efficiency
*7 comments - 71% negative, 29% positive*

A few patients complain about poor organization, noting that requests take too long and complaints are often ignored.

> "Nothing gets done accordingly."
>
> "Nothing is easy or straight-forward and so time consuming"

### Staff
*187 comments - 46% negative, 53% positive*

Many patients praise the staff for being kind, caring, and supportive, noting that they often go above and beyond to help. However, many other patients express frustration over severe staff shortages, which lead to canceled activities and a lack of support. Some patients also raise concerns about inexperienced or unprofessional staff who do not seem to care about patient well-being.

#### Staff manner & personal attributes
*127 comments - 23% negative, 76% positive*

Many patients commend the staff for their friendly, respectful, and caring attitudes, though some feel certain staff can be provoking or dismissive.

> "Staff are exceptionally kind."
>
> "Stop belittling people and calling them names."

#### Staffing levels & responsiveness
*71 comments - 85% negative, 14% positive*

Many patients report that low staffing levels negatively impact their care, resulting in fewer activities, less time out of their rooms, and rushed interactions.

> "We need more staff."
>
> "due to low staffing levels."

#### Competence & training
*12 comments - 75% negative, 25% positive*

A few patients feel that some staff lack the necessary experience or training to properly support their specific needs.

> "Too many inexperienced staff, who are sometime unsure of what they're supposed to be doing."
>
> "Improve staff awareness, training, and support for transgender healthcare."

### Communication & involvement
*154 comments - 44% negative, 56% positive*

Many patients appreciate staff who take the time to listen, understand their problems, and involve them in their care plans. Conversely, many patients feel ignored, stating that their concerns are not taken seriously and that communication is often poor. Some patients also request better, more consistent information regarding their treatment, appointments, and ward rules.

#### Staff listening, understanding & involving patients
*104 comments - 40% negative, 60% positive*

Many patients feel supported when staff actively listen and involve them in decisions, but many others feel dismissed or misunderstood by doctors and ward staff.

> "Listening to our needs and problems and taking them seriously"
>
> "Listen to me and don't judge. Treat me as an individual"

#### Information provision & guidance
*17 comments - 35% negative, 65% positive*

Some patients appreciate the educational materials and advice provided, while others feel there is a lack of information, particularly regarding specific healthcare needs.

> "All the info about my illness, so I understood it better."
>
> "No information on Transgender"

#### Being kept informed, clarity & consistency of information
*15 comments - 60% negative, 40% positive*

Some patients express frustration over inconsistent information and broken promises regarding their care and appointments.

> "Consistency in relaying information across the board of mental health practitioners and patients."
>
> "Make sure you follow up on things that are promised to you, a lot of the stuff talked about does not materialise and often left without reason"

#### Information directly from staff during care
*12 comments - 33% negative, 67% positive*

A few patients value when staff clearly explain their treatment and provide good advice, though some feel rules and complaints procedures are not explained well enough.

> "Everything was explained clearly"
>
> "Explain rules about ward to patients (eg entitlement, shop, order, money)."

#### Unspecified communication
*12 comments - 75% negative, 25% positive*

A few patients highlight a general need for better communication between staff and patients.

> "Communication, communication, communication."
>
> "Better communication with patients"

### Activities
*103 comments - 45% negative, 54% positive*

Many patients enjoy the variety of activities available, such as the gym, cooking, and horticulture, which help keep them busy and positive. However, many patients complain about a lack of activities, boredom, and restricted access to fresh air, often attributing this to staff shortages. Some patients also request better access to electronic entertainment, the internet, and educational courses.

#### Activities & access to fresh air
*92 comments - 41% negative, 58% positive*

Many patients value the therapeutic benefits of gym sessions, walks, and group activities, but many others feel trapped on the ward with too few opportunities to go outside or engage in meaningful tasks.

> "gym and fresh air walks a significant benefit"
>
> "Being stuck in my room and not being able to access activities"

#### Electronic entertainment
*15 comments - 60% negative, 40% positive*

Some patients appreciate having access to TVs and gaming consoles, while others request better access to the internet, music, and modern technology.

> "using x-box in my room."
>
> "There's no TV, no music, to stimulate your mind."

### Environment, equipment & catering
*71 comments - 54% negative, 44% positive*

Some patients are satisfied with the food, the coffee bar, and the general facilities provided. However, many patients complain about the quality and portion sizes of the food, requesting healthier options and access to the patient shop. Some patients also raise concerns about the physical environment, particularly the lack of natural light and dignity in seclusion rooms, as well as excessive noise on the wards.

#### Food & drink provision & facilities
*44 comments - 45% negative, 52% positive*

Some patients enjoy the meals and self-catering options, but many others criticize the food quality and ask for better dietary choices and the reopening of the shop.

> "Food is getting better."
>
> "Better food and more options."

#### Environment, facilities & equipment
*26 comments - 62% negative, 35% positive*

Some patients feel the hospital environment is supportive, but others complain about outdated seclusion rooms, lack of space, and unresolved maintenance issues.

> "Improve the seclusion rooms, there to old fashioned. You can't was, use the shower."
>
> "The workmen rarely fix problems in rooms"

#### Sensory experience
*5 comments - 80% negative, 20% positive*

A few patients report that the wards can be too noisy and that seclusion areas lack natural lighting.

> "Sometimes the ward is too noisy."
>
> "natural lighting in women's seclusion."

### Access to medical care & support
*58 comments - 79% negative, 21% positive*

Many patients express severe frustration with the long waiting times to see doctors, dentists, and mental health professionals. Missed or delayed appointments are a common complaint, leaving some patients in pain or without necessary medication. A few patients, however, report quick and reliable access to healthcare services when requested.

#### Appointment arrangements
*32 comments - 88% negative, 13% positive*

Many patients complain about infrequent appointments, long waiting lists, and poor scheduling for medical and dental care.

> "Get doctors appointment and dentist appointments more regular, so we don't have to wait months for an appointment."
>
> "missed appointments."

#### Timeliness of care
*30 comments - 73% negative, 27% positive*

Many patients are dissatisfied with how long it takes to receive treatment or be seen by a specialist, though a few note that their requests were handled quickly.

> "There could be a quicker way to see a doctor and start treatment sooner."
>
> "waiting times for appointments. Some people have to wait a long time to see a GP or dentist"

### Mental health specifics
*33 comments - 70% negative, 30% positive*

Many patients express frustration with the restrictions placed upon them, desiring more freedom, unescorted leave, and a quicker pathway out of the hospital. Some patients feel the rules are overly bureaucratic and that they are unfairly detained or restricted. A few patients appreciate the support they receive in preparing for release or moving to lower security.

#### Mental Health Act
*33 comments - 70% negative, 30% positive*

Many patients feel trapped by the strict security measures and request more leave, freedom, and a faster transition out of the secure environment.

> "Give us more freedom, I consider myself a trapped person."
>
> "Allow us more freedoms, it just seemed so unnecessary to be under so many security measures."

### Patient journey & service coordination
*28 comments - 54% negative, 46% positive*

Some patients appreciate the consistency of their care and the support they receive when transitioning between services or preparing for discharge. However, many patients complain about a lack of continuity, citing frequent staff changes and poor communication between different agencies. Some patients also express a strong desire to be discharged or moved off the ward more quickly.

#### Continuity of care
*22 comments - 45% negative, 55% positive*

Some patients value having a consistent care plan and named nurse, while others are frustrated by high staff turnover and disjointed inter-agency working.

> "Staff/doctors not being here long enough for my treatment, no consistency."
>
> "Care plans (nursing) are overly complicated/convoluted. Cross-sectoral/inter-agency working eg. with Arnold Lodge & the Assertive Transition service (ATS) is not seamless & communication is not smooth between organisation ot the detriment of patient care."

#### Discharge
*6 comments - 83% negative, 17% positive*

A few patients express a strong desire to go home or be moved on to the next stage of their pathway more quickly.

> "moving patients quicker to moving on"
>
> "Quicker pathway out of Rampton."

### Medication & pain
*25 comments - 68% negative, 32% positive*

Some patients report that their medication is helpful and that staff manage their prescriptions well. However, many patients complain about delays in receiving medication, inconsistent delivery times, and feeling forced to take drugs they do not want. A few patients also express distress over vital medications being stopped without proper consultation.

#### Supplying & understanding medication
*23 comments - 65% negative, 35% positive*

Many patients are frustrated by the slow or inconsistent supply of medication, and some feel their preferences regarding medication are ignored.

> "The only thing that I think could improve is the consistency with the time IDTS is deliveries. I believe this could be improved by delivering IDTS early morning and at the same time everyday due to the nature of the medication."
>
> "Work with patients who do not want to take medication."
